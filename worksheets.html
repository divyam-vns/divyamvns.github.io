2026 Neurodiversity-Affirming Approach

All worksheets on this page reflect updated 2026 clinical best practices: strengths-based framing, individual autonomy, sensory-informed design, and co-regulation strategies. They are designed to support — not suppress — authentic autistic expression. Worksheets cover early childhood through adulthood.

CBT & Emotional Regulation

2026 Guideline note: The latest autism-adapted CBT frameworks (2025–2026) integrate polyvagal theory, interoception awareness, and co-regulation. These worksheets use concrete, visual, and body-based approaches rather than purely cognitive techniques. Developed in consultation with autistic self-advocates.
