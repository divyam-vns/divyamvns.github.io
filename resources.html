Crisis & helplines

📞

Autism Response Team (Autism Speaks) — USA

1-888-AUTISM2 (1-888-288-4762) · autismresponse@autismspeaks.org · Mon–Fri 9am–5pm ET. Bilingual staff (English & Spanish). For families who need immediate guidance, resources, or referrals.

📞

Autism Helpline — National Autistic Society (UK)

0808 800 4104 · Mon–Thu 10am–4pm, Fri 9am–3pm. Free advice and information for autistic people and their families throughout the UK.

📞

Crisis Text Line — USA / Canada / UK / Ireland

Text HOME to 741741 (USA) · Text HELLO to 85258 (UK) · 24/7, free, confidential. For autistic individuals or family members in emotional distress.
