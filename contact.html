Quick answers
How do I download worksheets? +
Go to the Education page and use the filters to find resources. Click "Download PDF" on any card to download the file directly to your device.
Can I submit my own resources? +
Yes! Use the contact form on this page to submit your resources for review. We welcome worksheets, guides, translated materials, and research links.
Are resources available in other languages? +
We are actively working on translated materials. Use the language selector in the navigation to switch the site language. Contact us if you'd like to help translate resources.
How do I find autism support near me? +
Visit the Resources page for links to global autism organizations. You can also contact us with your location and we'll help you find local support.
